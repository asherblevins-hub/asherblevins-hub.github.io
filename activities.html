Activities Page

Texas A&M Game Developers

Regarding TAGD, the Texas A&M Game Developers club, I started attending meetings in Fall of my Freshman year. After a year of attending every meeting, becoming a member, and consistently engaging in the community, I decided I would take my chance, and run for the position of Treasurer for the club. I was then elected to a year’s term, and a reelection, where I’ve had the opportunity to teach game design concepts to the club, manage the finances, and be a greater part of club operations.

Chillennium

As the largest in-person college game jam in the world, I was enticed into competing at Chillennium my first 2 years in college. These experiences were foundational to my understanding of game design and collaboration. This upcoming year, I wanted to put my experience both with the gaming community and managing finances to use by becoming the Chillennium Sponsorship officer. Through this position, I am in charge of acquiring funds for the event, managing the storefront, and managing the budget. I am also in charge of a small group of dedicated committee members, alongside my co-officer.

Summer Camps

As both a method to further my personal understanding of game development and design, I have organized teaching material for, and personally taught several summer camps for middle to high school students. All of these experiences have been week long sessions, where I start with an educational presentation each day, and then break the students into groups to work on the day's topic. Throughout the week, the students will also have the opportunity to work on a long-term collaborative game project, where they compete for several award categories.
